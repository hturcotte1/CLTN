Very minor points

1. The two-parameter boundary list omits oﬀ-support curves (Section 3.1, page 8).
The paragraph beginning “The two-parameter version” says that the SFP verdict is invariant on
connected components of the complement of ai = 0, D = 0, ∆m−1 = 0 and L = 0. For a proper
support σ, this assertion fails: the outside-neuron condition can change sign without crossing any
of the listed curves. Lemma 3.2 and the later deﬁnition of Z include the polynomials ok for k ∈/ σ,
but this opening list does not.
For a concrete example, let σ = {1, . . . , 6} induce G1 from Table 1, and add vertex 7 receiving edges
from exactly 2, 3, 5, 6, with no outgoing edges. Set t = 100 and let β range over [52/25, 209/100].
Throughout this segment, all six ai , D, all six Hurwitz determinants and L are strictly positive.
Exact root counts give no zero of any of these polynomials in the segment, and their endpoint signs
are positive. The segment therefore lies in one connected component of the complement of the
listed curves.
However, the outside-neuron numerator is

                    o7 (β) = d − β(a1 + a4 ) = −β 5 + 3β 4 − 3β 3 + 3β 2 − 2β + 1,

and its endpoint values are

Since D > 0, these are also the signs of the outside drive. Thus σ is not an SFP at the ﬁrst endpoint
and is an SFP at the second, contradicting the stated invariance.
This is a local omission in the written statement for proper supports. The reconstructed set
later in the paragraph uses the complete Z. Full supports have no outside-neuron conditions, so
this example does not establish an error in the full-support counts or threshold values, or in the
implementation.
